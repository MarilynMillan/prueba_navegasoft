POS Combo Products

Sell products swiftly in Odoo POS!

POS Combo Products allows you to create product combos in Odoo POS. You can create and view POS combo groups and POS combo products. Moreover, you can configure between grid and list view to select items. Also, the module allows category and combo configuration in the Odoo backend.

Need for POS Combo Products

Occasionally, customers buy certain products in combos, such as tables and chairs, pens and notebooks, etc., especially in restaurants and franchises. Customers want to purchase combos because it's effortless, time-saving, and more affordable.

We understood this need and created Odoo POS Combo Products. This Odoo app allows you to configure and add combo products in Odoo POS. Also, the module lets you show combo products in a list or grid view. You can configure the maximum combo options for a product and much more.

Perks of POS Combo Products

  • The POS Combo Products increase sales and conversion rate.
  • This combo product module allows you to know customer preferences.
  • Shows inventory and non-inventory items in the combo selection popup.
  • It allows product inventory management with a tick in the Odoo backend.
  • You can make different combos from the available products in Odoo POS.

How Combo Products Increase Sales?

Combo products increase the average order value. It influences customers to buy more than one product in a single purchase order. Also, it ensures less wastage of products. The customer also gets several products customized according to their need.

It combines multiple products into one, and you can sell them as one unit in Odoo POS.
